Download

Get Jarvis for Windows

BETA

Jarvis v

Windows 10 / 11 · 64-bit · free

Download installer (.exe)
  1. Install Ollama (free, runs the AI).
  2. Run Jarvis-Setup.exe and follow the installer.
  3. Open Jarvis and click "Sign in with browser" (Google or email).
  4. First start downloads the AI model (~6 GB). After that, say "Hey Jarvis".

Windows SmartScreen may warn you because the app isn't code-signed yet. Click More info → Run anyway.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • 16 GB RAM (8 GB works, slower)
  • NVIDIA GPU with 6+ GB VRAM recommended
  • ~10 GB free disk space
  • Microphone (and webcam for scanning)
  • Internet for the voice and web search

FAQ

Questions

Is Jarvis free?

Yes. The AI runs on your own PC through Ollama, so there are no monthly costs.

Does it send my conversations anywhere?

No. Your voice is transcribed on your PC and the AI runs locally. Only web searches and the speaking voice use the internet. Your account only stores your email and which PCs are linked.

Can it break my computer?

Jarvis asks before anything risky (deleting, shutting down, running commands), sends deleted files to the Recycle Bin, refuses to touch system folders and never closes apps on your protected list.

Windows or my antivirus warns about Jarvis. Is it safe?

Yes. Jarvis is open source: every line of code is public on GitHub, and each download lists its SHA-256 fingerprint so you can check the file wasn't changed. New apps without a paid code-signing certificate get a "Windows protected your PC" notice until enough people have installed them; click More info → Run anyway. Some antivirus programs (for example Avast or 360) also scan new apps for a few seconds while they install; the installer waits for that automatically.

Does the world map show where I live?

No. Each linked PC reports only its country, rounded to a whole map region. No city, address or personal details are ever shown.

Which languages does it speak?

Jarvis answers in the language you talk to it in, with a native voice for over 25 languages including Slovak, Czech, German and Polish.

My PC has no NVIDIA GPU. Will it work?

Yes, but answers are slower because the AI runs on the CPU. You can pick a smaller model in Settings.
